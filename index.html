Welcome to Bryant's Dose Strength Calculator

This calculator helps determine how much liquid is needed when changing the concentration (strength) of a medication while keeping the total amount of medication constant.

How It Works

  1. Original Strength — Current medication concentration.
  2. Original Liquid Amount — Current liquid volume.
  3. New Strength — Desired concentration.

The calculator automatically determines the new liquid amount needed to maintain the same total quantity of medication.

Formula

New Liquid Amount = Original Liquid Amount × (Original Strength ÷ New Strength)

Common Uses

  • Medication dilution calculations
  • Compounding and formulation adjustments
  • Research and laboratory preparations
  • Strength conversion calculations
  • Volume adjustment planning
Important:
This calculator is intended as a mathematical reference tool only. Always verify medication concentrations and dosing instructions using appropriate medical, veterinary, pharmaceutical, or laboratory guidelines. Do not use its result as a substitute for professional dosing instructions.

About This Calculator

This tool applies an inverse-proportion formula to estimate a liquid volume that would keep the calculated total quantity constant when concentration changes.

New Liquid Amount = Original Liquid Amount × (Original Strength ÷ New Strength)

For meaningful results: use matching units and comparable concentration values. The calculator does not verify a medication, validate a prescribed dose, or account for product-specific instructions.

Safety reminder: This is a mathematical reference tool, not medical advice. Do not change, dilute, compound, or administer medication based only on this result. Confirm all calculations with a qualified healthcare professional or pharmacist and follow the product's official instructions.
